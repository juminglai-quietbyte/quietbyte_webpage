Privacy Policy

SeattleHub by QuietByte

Effective Date: April 2, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds SeattleHub, a free community resource app for finding activities for seniors and kids in the Seattle area. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

SeattleHub does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, SeattleHub:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties for marketing or profiling
  • Does NOT transmit any data to external servers

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only using SwiftData:

  • Saved/favorited activities
  • App preferences (filters, language)

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Location Services

SeattleHub includes a map view that shows activity locations. The app uses MapKit to display activity pins on a map. Location data is processed entirely on your device by Apple's MapKit framework. We do not collect, store, or transmit your location data.

Activity Data

All activity listings (organizations, addresses, phone numbers, schedules) are bundled within the app from publicly available community resources. No data is fetched from external servers at runtime.

Permissions

SeattleHub does not require any special device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or any other sensitive device features.

Third-Party Services

SeattleHub does not connect to any external services beyond Apple's MapKit for map rendering. The app functions entirely offline with no network calls to our servers.

In-App Purchases

SeattleHub is completely free. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid features.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Since all data is stored locally on your device, there is no data to share.

Children's Privacy

SeattleHub is designed to help families find activities for children and seniors. The app does not collect any personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@quietbyte.io.

中文

隐私政策

SeattleHub 西雅图生活圈 — QuietByte 出品

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 2 日

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了 SeattleHub（西雅图生活圈），一款免费的社区资源应用，帮助用户查找西雅图地区适合老人和儿童的活动。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

信息收集

SeattleHub 不会为了分析、广告或跟踪目的而收集、存储或共享个人信息。具体而言，SeattleHub：

  • 不要求注册或登录账户
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据用于营销或用户画像
  • 不向外部服务器传输任何数据

本地数据存储

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地（使用 SwiftData）：

  • 收藏的活动
  • 应用偏好设置（筛选条件、语言）

这些数据不会离开您的设备，不会传输至任何服务器，删除应用后将被一并删除。

定位服务

SeattleHub 包含一个地图视图，用于显示活动地点。该应用使用 Apple 的 MapKit 在地图上显示活动标记。位置数据完全在您的设备上由 Apple 的 MapKit 框架处理。我们不收集、存储或传输您的位置数据。

活动数据

所有活动列表（机构、地址、电话号码、时间表）均来自公开的社区资源，内置于应用中。应用运行时不会从外部服务器获取数据。

权限

SeattleHub 不需要任何特殊的设备权限。它不会访问您的相机、麦克风、通讯录或任何其他敏感设备功能。

第三方服务

SeattleHub 除了使用 Apple 的 MapKit 渲染地图外，不连接任何外部服务。该应用不会向我们的服务器发起任何网络请求。

应用内购买

SeattleHub 完全免费。没有应用内购买、订阅或付费功能。

数据共享

我们不与任何第三方共享数据。由于所有数据都存储在您的设备本地，因此没有数据可供共享。

儿童隐私

SeattleHub 旨在帮助家庭查找适合儿童和老人的活动。该应用不会收集儿童或任何其他用户的个人信息。

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 info@quietbyte.io 与我们联系。
