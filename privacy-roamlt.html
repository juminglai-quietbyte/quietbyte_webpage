Privacy Policy

Roam LT by QuietByte

Effective Date: April 7, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds Roam LT, a travel data usage navigator that helps you manage mobile data while traveling. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

Roam LT does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, Roam LT:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties for marketing or profiling
  • Does NOT transmit any data to external servers

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only using SwiftData:

  • Trip configurations (data limits, duration, destination)
  • Estimated data usage statistics
  • Travel preparation checklist progress
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network Usage Detection

Roam LT uses Apple's Network framework (NWPathMonitor) to detect whether your device is connected to WiFi. This is used solely to pause data tracking while on WiFi. No network information is collected, stored, or transmitted.

Cellular Data Estimation

Roam LT reads aggregate cellular interface statistics available through standard iOS APIs to estimate your data usage. This data is processed entirely on your device. Per-app usage breakdowns shown in the app are estimates only — iOS does not provide real per-app data tracking to third-party apps.

Widgets, Live Activities & Apple Watch

Roam LT offers home screen widgets, lock screen widgets, Live Activities (Dynamic Island), and an Apple Watch companion app. These features share trip data through Apple's App Groups, which keeps data on your device. No data is sent to external servers.

Permissions

Roam LT uses the following device capability:

  • Local Network: Used to detect WiFi connection status only

Roam LT does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location.

In-App Purchases

Roam LT offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade processed through Apple's App Store. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

Roam LT does not connect to any external services. The app functions entirely on-device with no network calls to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Roam LT does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@quietbyte.io.

中文

隐私政策

Roam LT 旅行流量助手 — QuietByte 出品

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 7 日

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了 Roam LT，一款帮助您在旅行中管理移动数据使用的智能助手。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

信息收集

Roam LT 不会为了分析、广告或跟踪目的而收集、存储或共享个人信息。具体而言，Roam LT：

  • 不要求注册或登录账户
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据用于营销或用户画像
  • 不向外部服务器传输任何数据

本地数据存储

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地（使用 SwiftData）：

  • 旅行配置（流量限额、天数、目的地）
  • 估算的数据使用统计
  • 旅行准备清单进度
  • 应用偏好设置

这些数据不会离开您的设备，不会传输至任何服务器，删除应用后将被一并删除。

网络使用检测

Roam LT 使用 Apple 的网络框架（NWPathMonitor）来检测您的设备是否连接了 WiFi。这仅用于在 WiFi 环境下暂停流量追踪。不会收集、存储或传输任何网络信息。

蜂窝数据估算

Roam LT 通过标准 iOS API 读取蜂窝网络接口的汇总统计数据，以估算您的数据使用量。这些数据完全在您的设备上处理。应用中显示的每应用使用量分析为估算值——iOS 不允许第三方应用获取真实的每应用数据追踪。

小组件、实时活动和 Apple Watch

Roam LT 提供主屏幕小组件、锁屏小组件、实时活动（灵动岛）和 Apple Watch 配套应用。这些功能通过 Apple 的 App Groups 共享旅行数据，数据保留在您的设备上，不会发送至外部服务器。

权限

Roam LT 使用以下设备功能：

  • 本地网络：仅用于检测 WiFi 连接状态

Roam LT 不会访问您的相机、麦克风、通讯录、照片或位置信息。

应用内购买

Roam LT 提供可选的一次性专业版升级，通过 Apple App Store 处理。购买交易完全由 Apple 处理，我们不收集或存储任何支付信息。

第三方服务

Roam LT 不连接任何外部服务。该应用完全在设备上运行，不会向我们的服务器发起任何网络请求。

儿童隐私

Roam LT 不会收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 info@quietbyte.io 与我们联系。
