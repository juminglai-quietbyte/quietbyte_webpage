Privacy Policy

PillowBook (梦织坊) by QuietByte

Effective Date: April 7, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds PillowBook (梦织坊), an AI-powered bedtime story app for families. We are committed to protecting your privacy and your family's privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

PillowBook does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, PillowBook:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties for marketing or profiling

AI Story Generation

PillowBook uses AI to generate personalized bedtime stories. To create a story, limited information (such as the child's name and a brief description of their day) may be sent to our server. This information is used solely to generate the story and is not stored, logged, or retained on the server after the story is returned to your device.

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Child profiles (name, preferences)
  • Generated stories
  • App settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device (except for the limited story generation request described above), is not transmitted to any server for storage, and is deleted when you delete the app.

In-App Purchases

PillowBook may offer optional in-app purchases. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

PillowBook does not integrate any analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or other third-party tracking tools. The only external connection is for AI story generation as described above.

Children's Privacy

PillowBook is designed for families to use together. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • No personal data is stored on our servers
  • Information sent for story generation is not retained after the story is returned
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising is used

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@quietbyte.io.

隐私政策

梦织坊 — QuietByte 出品

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 7 日

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了梦织坊，一款面向家庭的 AI 睡前故事应用。我们致力于保护您和您家人的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

信息收集

梦织坊不会为了分析、广告或跟踪目的而收集、存储或共享个人信息。具体而言，梦织坊：

  • 不要求注册或登录账户
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据用于营销或用户画像

AI 故事生成

梦织坊使用 AI 来生成个性化的睡前故事。为了创建故事，有限的信息（如孩子的名字和当天活动的简要描述）可能会被发送至我们的服务器。这些信息仅用于生成故事，在故事返回到您的设备后，不会在服务器上存储、记录或保留。

本地数据存储

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地：

  • 孩子的个人资料（名字、偏好）
  • 已生成的故事
  • 应用设置和偏好

除上述有限的故事生成请求外，这些数据不会离开您的设备，不会传输至任何服务器进行存储，删除应用后将被一并删除。

应用内购买

梦织坊可能提供可选的应用内购买。所有交易均由 Apple 通过 App Store（StoreKit）处理。我们不会接收、存储或访问您的支付信息。

第三方服务

梦织坊不集成任何分析 SDK、广告框架或其他第三方跟踪工具。唯一的外部连接用于上述 AI 故事生成。

儿童隐私

梦织坊是为家庭共同使用而设计的。我们高度重视儿童隐私：

  • 我们的服务器不存储任何个人数据
  • 为生成故事而发送的信息，在故事返回后不会被保留
  • 不使用任何分析、跟踪或广告服务

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 privacy@quietbyte.io 与我们联系。
