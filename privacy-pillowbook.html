Privacy Policy

Pillowbook by QuietByte

Effective Date: March 31, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds Pillowbook, a bedtime story app for families. We are committed to protecting your privacy and your family's privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

Pillowbook does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, Pillowbook:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties for marketing or profiling

AI Story Generation

Pillowbook uses AI (OpenAI GPT-4o mini, accessed through a Cloudflare Workers proxy at pillowbook-api.quietbyte.workers.dev) to generate personalized bedtime stories. To create a story, the following information is sent to our server:

  • The child's name
  • A brief description of the child's daily events (as entered by the parent)

This information is used solely to generate the story and is not stored, logged, or retained on the server after the story is returned to your device. The Cloudflare Workers proxy does not log or store any request data.

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only using SwiftData:

  • Child profiles (name, preferences)
  • Generated stories
  • Family photos
  • App settings and preferences

Photos are stored locally only and are never uploaded to any server.

This data never leaves your device (except for the limited story generation request described above), is not transmitted to any server for storage, and is deleted when you delete the app.

In-App Purchases

Pillowbook may offer optional in-app purchases. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

Pillowbook connects to a single external service for story generation:

  • Cloudflare Workers proxy (pillowbook-api.quietbyte.workers.dev) that forwards story generation requests to OpenAI. No data is logged or stored by this proxy.

Pillowbook does not integrate any analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or other third-party tracking tools.

Children's Privacy

Pillowbook is designed for families to use together. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • No personal data is stored on our servers
  • Child names and daily events sent for story generation are not retained after the story is returned
  • Photos remain on the device and are never uploaded
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising is used

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@quietbyte.io.

隐私政策

枕边书 (Pillowbook) — QuietByte 出品

生效日期：2026 年 3 月 31 日

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了枕边书，一款面向家庭的睡前故事应用。我们致力于保护您和您家人的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

信息收集

枕边书不会为了分析、广告或跟踪目的而收集、存储或共享个人信息。具体而言，枕边书：

  • 不要求注册或登录账户
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据用于营销或用户画像

AI 故事生成

枕边书使用 AI（OpenAI GPT-4o mini，通过 Cloudflare Workers 代理 pillowbook-api.quietbyte.workers.dev 访问）来生成个性化的睡前故事。为了创建故事，以下信息会被发送至我们的服务器：

  • 孩子的名字
  • 家长输入的孩子当天活动的简要描述

这些信息仅用于生成故事，在故事返回到您的设备后，不会在服务器上存储、记录或保留。Cloudflare Workers 代理不会记录或存储任何请求数据。

本地数据存储

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地（使用 SwiftData）：

  • 孩子的个人资料（名字、偏好）
  • 已生成的故事
  • 家庭照片
  • 应用设置和偏好

照片仅存储在本地，绝不会上传至任何服务器。

除上述有限的故事生成请求外，这些数据不会离开您的设备，不会传输至任何服务器进行存储，删除应用后将被一并删除。

应用内购买

枕边书可能提供可选的应用内购买。所有交易均由 Apple 通过 App Store（StoreKit）处理。我们不会接收、存储或访问您的支付信息。

第三方服务

枕边书仅连接一个外部服务用于故事生成：

  • Cloudflare Workers 代理（pillowbook-api.quietbyte.workers.dev），将故事生成请求转发至 OpenAI。该代理不会记录或存储任何数据。

枕边书不集成任何分析 SDK、广告框架或其他第三方跟踪工具。

儿童隐私

枕边书是为家庭共同使用而设计的。我们高度重视儿童隐私：

  • 我们的服务器不存储任何个人数据
  • 为生成故事而发送的孩子名字和日常活动描述，在故事返回后不会被保留
  • 照片保留在设备上，绝不会被上传
  • 不使用任何分析、跟踪或广告服务

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 privacy@quietbyte.io 与我们联系。
