Privacy Policy

ThoughtPoof by QuietByte

Effective Date: April 23, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds ThoughtPoof, a 60-second thought dump app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

ThoughtPoof does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, ThoughtPoof:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties for marketing or profiling
  • Does NOT make any network calls

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only using UserDefaults:

  • Saved thought entries (only if you choose to save)
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

ThoughtPoof does not require any special device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device features.

Third-Party Services

ThoughtPoof does not connect to any external services. The app functions entirely offline with no network calls whatsoever.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Since all data is stored locally on your device and the app makes no network calls, there is no data to share.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@quietbyte.io.
