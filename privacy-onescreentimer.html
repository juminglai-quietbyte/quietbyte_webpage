Privacy Policy

One Screen Timer by QuietByte

Effective Date: March 31, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds One Screen Timer. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data — in short, we do not collect any personal data.

Information Collection

We do NOT collect any personal information.

One Screen Timer:

  • Does NOT collect personal data
  • Does NOT track user behavior
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties
  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT make any network requests

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only using SwiftData and UserDefaults:

  • Timer presets and settings
  • User preferences (appearance, sound)
  • In-app purchase status

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

In-App Purchases

One Screen Timer offers optional in-app purchases. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

Notifications

One Screen Timer may use local notifications (e.g., timer completion alerts). These are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any server. No notification data is transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

One Screen Timer does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising frameworks. The app makes zero network requests.

Children's Privacy

One Screen Timer is rated 4+ and is safe for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@quietbyte.io.

一屏专注 (One Screen Timer) — QuietByte 出品

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了一屏专注。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据——简而言之，我们不收集任何个人数据。

信息收集

我们不收集任何个人信息。

一屏专注：

  • 不收集个人数据
  • 不跟踪用户行为
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据
  • 不要求注册或登录账户

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地（使用 SwiftData 和 UserDefaults）：

通知

一屏专注可能使用本地通知（如计时完成提醒）。这些通知完全在您的设备上调度，不涉及任何服务器，通知数据不会被传输至任何地方。

第三方服务

一屏专注不集成任何第三方 SDK、分析工具或广告框架。应用不发起任何网络请求。

儿童隐私

一屏专注的年龄分级为 4+，适合儿童使用。我们不会有意收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。
