Privacy Policy

RulePad by QuietByte

Effective Date: April 11, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds RulePad, a measurement toolkit with screen rulers, AR measurement, protractor, and photo measurement. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

RulePad does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, RulePad:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties
  • Does NOT transmit any data to external servers

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Measurement history (distances, angles, areas)
  • Calibration settings
  • App preferences

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Camera Access

RulePad uses the device camera for AR (Augmented Reality) measurement. Camera data is processed entirely on-device in real-time using Apple's ARKit framework. No camera images or video are stored, transmitted, or shared. Camera access is optional — the screen ruler, protractor, and photo measurement features work without it.

Photo Library Access

RulePad accesses your photo library only when you choose to:

  • Import a photo for photo measurement
  • Save an AR measurement screenshot to your photo library

Photos are processed entirely on-device. No photos are uploaded or transmitted to any server.

In-App Purchases

RulePad offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade processed through Apple's App Store. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

RulePad does not connect to any external services. The app functions entirely on-device with no network calls.

Children's Privacy

RulePad does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@quietbyte.io.

中文

隐私政策

RulePad 测量工具箱 — QuietByte 出品

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 11 日

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了 RulePad，一款集屏幕尺子、AR测量、量角器和照片测量于一体的测量工具箱。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

信息收集

RulePad 不会为了分析、广告或跟踪目的而收集、存储或共享个人信息。具体而言：

  • 不要求注册或登录账户
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据
  • 不向外部服务器传输任何数据

本地数据存储

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地：

  • 测量历史（距离、角度、面积）
  • 校准设置
  • 应用偏好

这些数据不会离开您的设备，不会传输至任何服务器，删除应用后将被一并删除。

相机访问

RulePad 使用设备相机进行 AR（增强现实）测量。相机数据通过 Apple ARKit 框架完全在设备上实时处理。不会存储、传输或共享任何相机图像或视频。相机访问是可选的——屏幕尺子、量角器和照片测量功能不需要相机。

照片图库访问

RulePad 仅在您选择以下操作时访问照片图库：

  • 导入照片进行照片测量
  • 保存 AR 测量截图到照片图库

照片完全在设备上处理，不会上传或传输至任何服务器。

应用内购买

RulePad 提供可选的一次性专业版升级，通过 Apple App Store 处理。购买交易完全由 Apple 处理，我们不收集或存储任何支付信息。

第三方服务

RulePad 不连接任何外部服务。该应用完全在设备上运行，无网络请求。

儿童隐私

RulePad 不会收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 info@quietbyte.io 与我们联系。
