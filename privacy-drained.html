Privacy Policy

Drained by QuietByte

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds Drained, a battery tracking and logging app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

Drained does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, Drained:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties for marketing or profiling
  • Does NOT make any network calls

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only using UserDefaults:

  • Battery level readings
  • Battery log history
  • App preferences

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

Drained does not require any special device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device features.

Third-Party Services

Drained does not connect to any external services. The app functions entirely offline with no network calls whatsoever.

In-App Purchases

Drained does not currently offer any in-app purchases.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Since all data is stored locally on your device and the app makes no network calls, there is no data to share.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@quietbyte.io.

隐私政策

Drained — QuietByte 出品

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 1 日

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了 Drained，一款电池追踪和记录应用。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

信息收集

Drained 不会为了分析、广告或跟踪目的而收集、存储或共享个人信息。具体而言，Drained：

  • 不要求注册或登录账户
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据用于营销或用户画像
  • 不进行任何网络请求

本地数据存储

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地（使用 UserDefaults）：

  • 电池电量读数
  • 电池日志记录
  • 应用偏好设置

这些数据不会离开您的设备，不会传输至任何服务器，删除应用后将被一并删除。

权限

Drained 不需要任何特殊的设备权限。它不会访问您的相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录或任何其他敏感设备功能。

第三方服务

Drained 不连接任何外部服务。该应用完全离线运行，不进行任何网络请求。

应用内购买

Drained 目前不提供任何应用内购买。

数据共享

我们不与任何第三方共享数据。由于所有数据都存储在您的设备本地，且应用不进行任何网络请求，因此没有数据可供共享。

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 info@quietbyte.io 与我们联系。
