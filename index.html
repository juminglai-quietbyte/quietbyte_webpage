Notebook Games

Doodle. Play. Relax.

  • Tic Tac Toe with 3 AI difficulty levels
  • Dots & Boxes with smart AI opponent
  • Hand-drawn notebook paper aesthetic
  • Offline • No ads in gameplay • No account needed

FreshNest

Dinner, Handled.

A calm dinner companion for busy moms with toddlers. Tonight's dinner is already chosen, adapted for your little one, with a grocery list that writes itself. 30 days of Chinese home-style meals, bilingual English & Chinese.

  • Tonight's dinner decided before you open the app
  • Every meal adapted for toddlers — soft, low salt, right-sized
  • Auto-generated grocery list grouped by category
  • Fridge box tracker & weekly prep planner
  • Rescue Mode for the nights nothing sounds good
  • Beautiful light & dark mode

Sudoku Garden

Calm Puzzle

A calm, distraction-free Sudoku experience with soothing colors and smooth animations. Three difficulty levels, smart pencil marks, intelligent hints, and full statistics.

  • Beautiful light & dark mode themes
  • Works offline • No tracking • No clutter
