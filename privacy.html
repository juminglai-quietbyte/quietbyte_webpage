Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 6, 2026 • Last Updated: March 26, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy covers all apps published by QuietByte, including Sudoku Garden and FreshNest.

Information Collection

We do NOT collect any personal information.

Our apps:

  • Do NOT collect personal data
  • Do NOT track user behavior
  • Do NOT use analytics services
  • Do NOT contain advertisements
  • Do NOT share data with third parties
  • Do NOT require an account or login
  • Do NOT access your contacts, photos, or other personal information

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Game statistics (scores, completion rates, best times)
  • User preferences (sound settings, timer settings, dark mode)
  • Current game state
  • Meal plan rotation and scheduling preferences
  • Grocery list check-off state

This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

Our apps do not require any special device permissions — no location, camera, photo library, contacts, or microphone access.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are rated 4+ and are safe for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update this page and the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our feedback form.
