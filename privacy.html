Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 6, 2026 • Last Updated: March 27, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy covers all apps published by QuietByte, including FreshNest, Notebook Games, and Sudoku Garden.

Information Collection

We do NOT collect any personal information.

Our apps:

  • Do NOT collect personal data
  • Do NOT track user behavior
  • Do NOT use analytics services
  • Do NOT contain advertisements
  • Do NOT share data with third parties
  • Do NOT require an account or login
  • Do NOT access your contacts, photos, or other personal information

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Game statistics, scores, and preferences
  • Meal plan rotation, grocery lists, and fridge box state
  • User settings (appearance, sound, notifications)
  • In-app purchase status

This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

In-App Purchases

Some of our apps offer optional in-app purchases (subscriptions or one-time unlocks). These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

Notifications

FreshNest offers optional local notifications (dinner reminders). These are scheduled on your device and do not involve any server. No notification data is transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

Our apps do not require any special device permissions — no location, camera, photo library, contacts, or microphone access. Notification permission is requested only if you enable dinner reminders in FreshNest.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are rated 4+ and are safe for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update this page and the "Last Updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@quietbyte.io.
