Privacy Policy

Don't Open Yet by QuietByte

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

Overview

QuietByte ("we," "our," or "us") builds Don't Open Yet, a mindful screen time app that adds a countdown pause before your chosen apps open. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information Collection

Don't Open Yet does not collect, store, or share personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. Specifically, Don't Open Yet:

  • Does NOT require an account or login
  • Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • Does NOT contain advertisements
  • Does NOT share data with third parties for marketing or profiling
  • Does NOT make any network calls

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device only using UserDefaults and SwiftData:

  • App delay preferences (which apps to delay)
  • Countdown duration settings
  • Daily stats (delays survived and dismissed)
  • Streak data

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to any server, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

Don't Open Yet requires Screen Time permission (FamilyControls framework) to monitor and display countdown overlays before selected apps open. Screen Time data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted externally.

The app also uses Face ID for settings protection. Face ID data is handled entirely by Apple's Secure Enclave and is never accessible to the app.

Don't Open Yet does NOT access your camera, location, contacts, microphone, or any other sensitive device features beyond those listed above.

Third-Party Services

Don't Open Yet does not connect to any external services. The app functions entirely offline with no network calls whatsoever. There are no third-party SDKs included.

In-App Purchases

Don't Open Yet offers an optional Pro subscription ($1.99/month) managed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not process payments directly and do not have access to your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all payment-related data.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Since all data is stored locally on your device and the app makes no network calls, there is no data to share.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@quietbyte.io.

隐私政策

别急着打开 — QuietByte 出品

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 1 日

概述

QuietByte（以下简称"我们"）开发了"别急着打开"，一款在你选择的 App 打开前加入倒计时暂停的正念屏幕时间应用。我们致力于保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

信息收集

别急着打开不会为了分析、广告或跟踪目的而收集、存储或共享个人信息。具体而言，别急着打开：

  • 不要求注册或登录账户
  • 不使用分析或跟踪服务
  • 不包含广告
  • 不与第三方共享数据用于营销或用户画像
  • 不进行任何网络请求

本地数据存储

所有应用数据仅存储在您的设备本地（使用 UserDefaults 和 SwiftData）：

  • App 延迟偏好设置（选择延迟哪些 App）
  • 倒计时时长设置
  • 每日统计数据（坚持次数和跳过次数）
  • 连续天数记录

这些数据不会离开您的设备，不会传输至任何服务器，删除应用后将被一并删除。

权限

别急着打开需要屏幕时间权限（FamilyControls 框架），用于在选定 App 打开前监测并显示倒计时覆盖层。屏幕时间数据完全在您的设备上处理，绝不会传输到外部。

本应用还使用 Face ID 保护设置页面。Face ID 数据完全由 Apple 的安全隔区处理，应用无法访问。

别急着打开不会访问您的相机、位置、通讯录、麦克风或上述以外的其他敏感设备功能。

第三方服务

别急着打开不连接任何外部服务。该应用完全离线运行，不进行任何网络请求，也不包含任何第三方 SDK。

应用内购买

别急着打开提供可选的 Pro 订阅（每月 ¥12），完全通过 Apple App Store 管理。我们不直接处理支付，也无法访问您的支付信息。所有支付相关数据受 Apple 隐私政策管辖。

数据共享

我们不与任何第三方共享数据。由于所有数据都存储在您的设备本地，且应用不进行任何网络请求，因此没有数据可供共享。

政策变更

如果我们更新本政策，将修改上方的生效日期。建议您定期查看此页面。

联系我们
